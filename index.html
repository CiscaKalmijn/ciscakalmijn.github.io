About me

I am a mathematics PhD candidate at the Radboud Universiteit in Nijmegen. Under the supervision of Walter van Suijlekom, I work on notions such as entropy in noncommutative (differential) geometry. I am a member of the consortium Emergence at All Scales, see also https://www.d-iep.org/eaas.
